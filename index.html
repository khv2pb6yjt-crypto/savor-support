Common questions

A recipe won’t import

Try the original recipe webpage or paste the recipe text. Apple Intelligence is optional for websites with structured recipe data. Review imported ingredients, amounts, and instructions before saving. Very large pages may be rejected; paste the relevant text instead.

I can’t find a recipe

Open Recently deleted from the Recipes filter menu. Before restoring a backup, export your current library. Recovery copies are available in Settings → Recovery backups. Disconnect household sync before restoring.

My household isn’t syncing

Check your iCloud sign-in and network connection, then choose Sync now in Settings. Changes sync while Savor is open. Household owners can remove members or stop sharing using Manage household access. Disconnecting a device only stops that device’s sync.

Where is my cooking timer?

Reopen cooking mode from any recipe to view or cancel the active timer. It continues when you close cooking mode. Check Savor’s notification permission in iOS Settings if you did not receive an alert.

How do backups work?

Export a backup in Settings and keep a copy in Files. Backups include recipe photos and saved content. The app keeps the newest 10 local recovery copies; export ones you want to retain. Backup imports currently support files up to 100 MB. Contact support before changing or deleting data if your backup exceeds that size.

Are tips required?

No. Tips are optional and are processed by Apple. Apple manages payments and refund requests. For a refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Privacy policy

Savor is developed by Kevin Kent. This policy describes how Savor and this support website handle information.

Your saved content

Savor stores recipes, selected photos, meal plans, grocery lists, and cooking history on your device. You can use the local features without creating a Savor account. Savor contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Optional household sharing

If you connect a household, this content is synchronized through Apple’s CloudKit service using your iCloud account. Invited members can view and edit shared household content. The owner can remove members or stop sharing in Settings → Manage household access.

Disconnecting one device stops its synchronization; it does not revoke other members or erase copies they already hold. Local app data, shared iCloud content, and exported copies have separate lifecycles.

Website import and on-device intelligence

Website import requests the website you choose. That website receives normal network information, including your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. Where available, recipe text extraction and formatting use Apple’s on-device intelligence features. Recipe text is not sent to a developer-operated cloud AI service.

Permissions and purchases

Savor uses Apple’s photo picker for images you select. Reminders access is requested when you export groceries. Notification permission is requested for cooking timers. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Tips are optional purchases processed by Apple. Savor receives transaction information needed to handle purchases and does not receive your payment-card details.

Widgets and the share extension

Widgets and the share extension exchange recipe and planning information with the app through a local App Group. Widget content may be visible to people who can see your device.

Backups, retention, and deletion

Exports include your saved recipe content and photos. You choose their destination and control copies you share. The app retains up to 10 local recovery backups before operations such as restoration and household changes. You can export or delete those files in Settings → Recovery backups.

Deleting a recipe does not remove it from previously exported or recovery files. Uninstalling the app may remove local data, but does not necessarily delete shared iCloud content or exported copies. Device backups may also retain app data according to your Apple settings.

Optional diagnostics

Grocery diagnostics can store recipe titles and ingredient merge details locally. Recording is off by default. Turning it off deletes the saved diagnostic report. The app does not automatically send that report to the developer.

Support messages and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot. Messages are handled through Gmail and retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain relevant support records. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This site does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Policy changes and contact

Changes will be posted here with a revised update date. For privacy questions or requests, email SavorSupport@gmail.com and mention Savor.
